# Design System: myGroww

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Living Money Map"**

Money is drawn as a connected map. Cards are places, hairlines are the routes between them, and light travels those routes when money moves. The system exists to make one number trustworthy at a glance, what is safe to spend, by showing what feeds it. Everything outside the map stays deliberately ordinary: standard navigation, standard controls, sentence-case labels and plain rows, so the moving part keeps its meaning.

The world comes in two themes, Obsidian Gold and Midnight Emerald, each with a dark and a light mode, set by `data-theme` and `data-mode` on the root element. Every combination carries exactly one accent and one hero surface that stays dark and tinted even in light mode. Gold is the default world. Density is phone-first and calm: one clear thing per screen, with further detail behind a tap, a tab or a count. The light-mode grounds are recorded as built, and remain open to the owner's reaction.

The confirmed rejections are a rainbow palette, the dullness of a bank app, and a crowded single screen.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A node map on Home, joined by live canvas lines, over conventional controls everywhere else.
- One accent per theme, with status hues reserved for status.
- Display numerals at one weight, with the rupee sign supplied by dedicated faces.
- Soft rings and shallow ambient shadows rather than borders.
- Motion that reports a real change: a comet travels, a card lifts, the number counts.

## Colors

A near-monochrome ground lifted by a single metallic or mineral accent, with semantic roles that keep the same name in all four combinations.

Frontmatter tokens are named `<theme>-<mode>-<role>`. The same role name is the CSS custom property in `src/index.css`: `gold-dark-accent` is `--accent` under gold and dark. Build against the custom properties, never against a mode's literal value.

### Primary
- **Obsidian Gold** (#D9B26A on dark, #C9981F on light): fills the primary button, the Add button, the pace marker and selection highlight in the gold theme.
- **Midnight Emerald** (#4FD1A1 on dark, #0F6B52 on light): the same jobs in the emerald theme.
- **Accent Ink** (the `accent-ink` role): the accent tuned for reading, used for the current nav item, selected chip text, notice actions and text links. Accent text uses this role, never the fill.
- **Accent Wash** (the `accent-soft` role): the translucent accent behind notices, selected chips and the active rail item.
- **Comet** (the `comet` role): the light travelling on map lines. It follows the bright accent ink in dark modes and a deep accent in light modes, so it stays visible on either ground.

### Neutral
- **Grounds** (#0A0A0B and #F5F3EE gold, #06120E and #EFF3F1 emerald): the page. Gold grounds are neutral near-black and warm ivory; emerald grounds carry a green tint.
- **Surface and Raised Surface** (the `surface` and `surface-2` roles): cards take `surface`; inputs, tab tracks, keypad keys and meter tracks take `surface-2`. Light modes use pure white cards in both themes. Dark emerald cards stay green-tinted; dark gold cards stay neutral.
- **Ink and Muted Ink** (the `ink` and `mute` roles): primary text and all secondary text. Muted ink reads at 5.2:1 or better on every ground and surface.
- **Hairlines** (the `line` and `line-strong` roles): translucent ink for rings and dividers, and for ghost controls and drag handles. Map lines are drawn from the same ink value at 34% opacity at rest.
- **Hero Surface** (the `hero`, `hero-ink`, `hero-mute`, `hero-edge`, `hero-track` roles): the tinted dark panel behind the one headline number, with its own text and track colors. It is dark in light mode too.
- **Backdrop** (the `backdrop` token): a fixed black scrim behind sheets, the same in all four combinations.

### Status and data
- **Good, Warn, Bad** (the `good`, `warn`, `bad`, `bad-soft` roles): state only, on badges, over-budget bars, errors, destructive actions and urgent notices. In the emerald theme `good` shares the accent's hue, which is intended and also why positive state must not depend on color alone.
- **Chart One and Chart Two** (the `chart-1`, `chart-2` roles): the accent hue and a neutral. These are the only two series colors.
- **Shine and Drop** (the `shine`, `drop` roles): the top-edge highlight and the ambient shadow color.

### Named Rules

**The One Accent Rule.** Each theme has one accent. Good, warn and bad are status colors and do not count, so gold's green `good` is legitimate; a second decorative hue is not.

**The Hero Stays Dark Rule.** The hero surface is dark and tinted in every mode, and there is one on a screen.

**The One Hue Chart Rule.** Data is read by position and label. Charts use one hue and one neutral, and categories are never color-coded.

## Typography

**Display Font, gold:** Bodoni Moda, weight 500 only (with Noto Serif for the rupee sign, then Georgia, serif)
**Body Font, gold:** Hanken Grotesk, 400, 500, 600 (with Mukta for the rupee sign, then system-ui, sans-serif)
**Display and Body Font, emerald:** Sora, 400, 500, 600 (with Mukta for the rupee sign at 118% size, then system-ui, sans-serif)

**Character:** Gold pairs a high-contrast fashion serif for figures with a plain, friendly grotesque for everything else. Emerald uses one geometric sans throughout, so its hierarchy comes from size and weight alone.

All role sizes are shared between themes. Frontmatter roles without a suffix are the gold stack; the `-emerald` siblings differ only in family.

### Hierarchy
- **Hero number** (500, clamp 44 to 54px, line-height 1.04, tracking -0.015em): the single headline figure, inside the hero surface.
- **Amount entry** (500, 46px, 1.15; 36px on short touch screens): the figure being typed in the Add sheet.
- **Page title** (500, clamp 27 to 36px, 1.1): page headers and the brand on Home.
- **Figures** (500, 18 to 25px, about 1.15): stat values at 25, outcome tiles at 24, standard map tiles at 21, small map tiles at 18.
- **Section title** (body face, 600, 18px, 1.2): section headings inside a page.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.45): running text and rows. Inputs stay at 16px so phones do not zoom on focus.
- **Small and tiny** (400, 14px and 13px): secondary lines, notices, tables, captions, legends.
- **Micro label** (12px): the smallest text in the system, on nav labels at 600, map tile sub-lines, chart labels and badges.

Labels are sentence case throughout. No uppercase treatment or wide tracking appears anywhere in the build. Amounts use Indian digit grouping, and figures declare tabular lining numerals so columns align and counting values do not jitter.

### Named Rules

**The Rupee Face Rule.** Bodoni Moda, Hanken Grotesk and Sora do not contain the rupee sign (U+20B9). Self-hosted faces supply only that character through `unicode-range`: Noto Serif latin-ext beside Bodoni Moda, Mukta latin-ext beside Hanken Grotesk, and Mukta at `size-adjust: 118%` beside Sora. Any stack that can show an amount keeps its rupee face directly after the primary family, so the sign is never left to a system fallback.

**The Single Display Weight Rule.** Only weight 500 of Bodoni Moda ships. Display text always uses the `--display-weight` property, so the browser never synthesizes a bold serif.

**The Display For Figures Rule.** The display face belongs to figures, page titles and the brand. Section titles, labels, buttons and running text stay in the body face.

## Layout

A phone-first shell. On phones, content sits in one column with a 16px gutter, above a fixed five-slot bottom bar whose centre slot is the Add button. At 900px and wider, the bar is replaced by a sticky 216px left rail, content padding becomes 26px 32px 48px, and the main column is capped at 1120px. Safe-area insets are honored at both the top and bottom edges.

Home's map is capped at 460px wide, with a 20px side channel that gives outer lines room to run past the cards. At 900px and wider, the map takes a 460px left column and supporting content the remainder, 32px apart. Other pages stack sections 22px apart, with auto-fit grids for paired cards (minimum 300px, 16px gap), stats (minimum 150px, 10px gap) and forms (minimum 150px, 12px gap).

Spacing is tight and follows reused steps rather than a strict scale; the frontmatter records those steps. Tap targets run from 40px on chips, small buttons and notice actions to 46px on standard buttons and fields, with 44px icon buttons and 52px or taller rows. On touch screens under 800px tall, the Add sheet compresses so the keypad and Save remain on screen.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid. Everything has a 1px ring drawn as an inset shadow with a thin top-edge shine, so surfaces read as cut from one material. Ambient drop shadows are reserved for things that float over the page or carry the map's story, and they use the `drop` color so they fade to almost nothing on light grounds and sink into dark ones.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card rest** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line), inset 0 1px 0 var(--shine)`): every standard card.
- **Map tile** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line), inset 0 1px 0 var(--shine), 0 10px 22px var(--drop)`): tiles on the map, deepening to `0 12px 26px` with a stronger ring on hover.
- **Hero tile** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--hero-edge), inset 0 1px 0 var(--shine), 0 16px 34px var(--drop)`): the deepest tile, with an accent-tinted ring.
- **Floating action** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px var(--drop)`): the round Add button.
- **Sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 -12px 40px var(--drop)`): the sheet, cast upward.
- **Toast** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 28px var(--drop)`): short confirmations, some offering Undo.
- **Hairline** (`box-shadow: 0 -1px 0 var(--line)`): dividers between rows and the edge of the nav bar.

### Named Rules

**The Ring Not Border Rule.** Cards, fields, chips and ghost buttons draw their edge with an inset shadow, so an edge never changes layout. The dashed goal route is the one true border, because it is a line and not an edge.

**The Earned Drop Rule.** A plain card has no drop shadow. Drops belong to map tiles, the Add button, sheets, toasts and the tab pill.

## Shapes

Soft, generous corners that step down with size: sheets at 24px, cards at 18px, map tiles at 16px, buttons at 14px, and fields, notices and icon buttons at 12px. Small buttons and chips sit at 11px, the tab track at 13px with a 10px pill inside. Meters and badges are fully rounded; the Add button, swatches and pace markers are circles. Map lines turn corners on a 12px quadratic curve, echoing the tile corners. Icons are inline SVG on a 24px grid, stroked at 1.8 with round caps and joins; the Home icon and the app icon both draw three joined nodes.

## Components

Conventional controls with precise press feedback, so that attention stays on the map.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (14px), 46px tall; the small variant is 11px and 40px.
- **Primary:** accent fill with `on-accent` text at 600.
- **Ghost:** no fill with a strong hairline ring. Danger keeps that form with `bad` text and ring.
- **States:** press scales to .97 in 140ms; hover on fine pointers brightens primary by 8% and fills ghost with `surface-2`; focus is a 2px `ring` outline offset by 2px; disabled is 50% opacity.
- **Icon button:** 44px square, muted ink, press scale .93.

### Chips
- **Style:** 40px tall at 11px radius with a strong hairline ring and 14px text at 500.
- **State:** selected takes the accent wash, accent ink, a 1.5px `ring` inset and weight 600, exposed through `aria-pressed`. Press scales to .96.

### Tabs
- **Style:** a `surface-2` track with equal segments. The selected segment gets a `surface` pill that slides between options on a short spring (320ms, bounce .12). Tab choice is kept in the address, so links can open a specific tab.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** softly rounded (18px).
- **Background:** `surface`.
- **Shadow Strategy:** card rest, as above.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px; list cards use 4px 16px with edge-to-edge row hover.
- **Stat card:** a muted label over a display figure. Stat grids belong on Activity, Plan and Wealth.
- **Connected cards:** every detail page ends with a "Connected to" list of live values linking to related pages, which carries the map idea beyond Home.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `surface-2` fill with a hairline ring, 12px radius, 46px tall, 16px text, with a 14px muted label above.
- **Focus:** a 2px `ring` outline at zero offset.
- **Keypad:** on touch devices the Add sheet uses a three-column keypad in `surface-2`; it is hidden on fine pointers, where the input is focused directly.

### Navigation
- **Bottom bar (phones):** `surface` with a hairline top, 12px labels at 600 under 24px icons, in muted ink with accent ink for the current page. The centre slot is a 54px round accent Add button.
- **Rail (900px and wider):** brand in the display face, 15px row items, accent wash on the current page, a full-width primary Add button, Settings pinned to the bottom.

### Money Map (signature)
Standard links arranged in a grid, with a decorative canvas of lines beneath them. The tile families are: a full-width Salary row at the top; small tiles for what leaves; a full-width hero; outcome tiles with a 3px meter. Lines are 1.25px, run between real tile edges, and recompute from layout boxes on resize.

- **Comets:** when a value changes, a comet (a 20px soft glow with a 34px tapered tail) travels the line that money actually moved through, at 380px per second with a 500ms minimum trip, while the line brightens and thickens, then cools. Comets start 200ms after Home sees the change, including a change made on another page, and they chain where money continues onward.
- **Resting light:** a fainter comet (a 9.5px glow at 70% opacity) drifts along lines of 70px or longer at 22px per second with a 140px pause. The direction contract asked for comets only on change; the build also runs them at rest to keep the map alive, and this is the recorded behavior.
- **Arrival lift:** when a comet lands, the destination tile lifts 4px and settles over 620ms.
- **Hero count:** the hero number holds its old value until its comet lands, then counts to the new one over 850ms.
- **First entrance:** on the first visit of a session, lines draw outward from Salary in sequence while tiles rise 12px and fade in over 620ms, staggered 70ms apart.
- **Card to title morph:** a tapped tile takes the `page-title` view-transition name and morphs into the destination page's title over 300ms, while the rest of the page crossfades in 170ms. Browsers without view transitions get a 260ms, 6px page entrance instead.

### Bottom Sheet (signature)
The single surface for quick entry and edits, 480px maximum width with 24px top corners and a 38px by 4px handle. It slides up over 280ms on `cubic-bezier(.32, .72, 0, 1)` with an 180ms backdrop fade. The handle and the sheet's header row drag it, with stiff resistance upward and loose resistance downward; release past 110px or above 500px per second dismisses it. Escape closes it and focus is trapped and returned. At 900px and wider it becomes a centred dialog with all four corners rounded, a scale and fade entrance, and no drag.

### Goal Route (signature)
A dashed line with two markers: a 2px bar for the date wanted and a 16px accent dot for the date at the current pace. The markers slide over 520ms as the plan changes, so a plan reads as distance rather than as a table of figures.

### Named Rules

**The Comet Tells The Truth Rule.** A bright event comet runs only on a line that the changed value passed through. Only the faint resting light is ambient.

**The Lines Decorate Rule.** The canvas is hidden from assistive tech and ignores pointers. Every tile is an ordinary link, and the page must work fully without the lines.

**The Motion Falls Back Rule.** Under reduced motion, lines appear complete, comets and lifts are skipped, numbers update at once, and all CSS motion collapses. No information may exist only in motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the role custom properties, so a component works in all four combinations without a mode check.
- **Do** check new surfaces in both themes and both modes; a gold-dark result does not prove the other three.
- **Do** use `--ease`, `cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1)`, for movement, with press feedback at about 140ms and color changes at 160ms.
- **Do** keep a rupee face in any font stack that can display an amount, and format figures with tabular lining numerals and Indian digit grouping.
- **Do** keep Home led by the map, with supporting items behind a count or below it, and let detail pages use ordinary stat grids and connected cards.
- **Do** run every new animation through the reduced-motion paths, in CSS and in script.

### Don't:
- **Don't** make the interface rainbow-coloured, dull like a bank app, or crowded on one screen.
- **Don't** add a second accent hue or color-code categories; status colors stay reserved for status.
- **Don't** put a second hero surface on a screen or lighten it in light mode.
- **Don't** set Bodoni Moda at any weight other than 500.
- **Don't** set body-face labels in uppercase or wide tracking; the build has neither.
- **Don't** add map lines that connect things money does not move between.
- **Don't** make a control depend on a canvas, hover or a comet to be understood.
