Scope: the myGroww app shell and its Home, Activity, Plan and Wealth screens. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: the owner first, then salaried Indians who support family and invest monthly. See what is safe to spend, log in seconds, plan goals.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Money as a connected map. The home screen is the app's cards joined by live lines, and money visibly travels along those lines when something changes. It refuses the category-default dashboard of equal stat tiles.
OWN-WORLD: Exactly one accent per theme, gold or emerald. Obsidian Gold sits on near-black or warm ivory with neutral cards. Midnight Emerald keeps its green-tinted ground and cards in dark, as shown in the four-theme comparison, where the owner said: "i loved Obsidian Gold Midnight Emerald both also keep dark n light mode". Light modes were requested but the owner has not yet seen them, so their grounds are open to the owner's reaction. Display numerals in Bodoni Moda (gold) or Sora (emerald). One hero card on a tinted dark surface in every mode.
STORY: Open the app, see what is safe to spend, see what feeds it, tap any card to go deeper, and every page links onward to connected cards.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: brand row, Salary across the top, Family and Bills, SIPs and Goals, the Safe to spend hero, then Safety net and top goal, joined by lines, with meters on the tiles. Bottom bar with Home, Activity, a central Add, Plan, Wealth. One notice below, with the rest behind a count.
FORM: Node map over standard controls; navigation and controls stay ordinary. The owner pinned this direction from a four-theme comparison, after an earlier roll. seed:064da983d3d068ed (rolled in a scratch copy of the project, so it cannot be corroborated from this folder)
SIGNATURE: Comets of light travel existing lines when values change, cards lift on arrival, the hero number counts when the comet lands, and a tapped card morphs into the title of its page.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
